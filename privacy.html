Privacy Policy

FeltIt for iOS · Last updated 23 October 2026

Summary

FeltIt does not operate any servers, does not require an account, and contains no advertising or third-party tracking code. Information you enter is stored on your device and, if iCloud sync is enabled, in the private database of your own Apple Account, which the developer cannot access.

FeltIt sends anonymous usage statistics to an analytics service, TelemetryDeck. They are enabled by default, may be disabled in the application, and never include your entries, notes, or Apple Health data. See section 4.

FeltIt reads data from Apple Health with your permission. It does not write to Apple Health. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing and is not disclosed to third parties.

1. Scope and contact

FeltIt is an iOS application developed and published by Emad Rahman, an independent developer based in Canada ("the developer"). This policy describes how the FeltIt iOS application and its Home Screen widgets handle information.

Enquiries regarding this policy may be sent to developer.emadrahman@gmail.com.

2. Information the application stores

FeltIt stores three categories of information, each generated on your device:

  • Information you enter. The body regions you record, the severity and sensation type you select, whether a region has been marked as resolved, any notes you write, and the date and time of each entry.
  • Health data you have authorised the application to read. Subject to your explicit permission, FeltIt reads six categories from Apple Health: workouts, sleep analysis, heart rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, respiratory rate, and step count. Access is requested on a read-only basis; the application does not write data to Apple Health.
  • Application settings. Preferences such as the selected theme, which insight sections are displayed, and which notifications are enabled.

The application does not request or store your name, email address, telephone number, date of birth, precise location, contacts, photographs, or any government or health-system identifier.

3. Storage locations

  • On the device. Information is stored in an application container on your iPhone, subject to iOS file protection and your device passcode. The Home Screen widgets read a summary of this information from the same on-device container.
  • In the iOS search index, for Siri and Spotlight. To allow your entries to be found in Spotlight and referred to in Siri requests, FeltIt adds a summary of each entry to the on-device search index maintained by iOS. The summary contains the date, the body regions, the highest severity, the sensation type, whether a region was marked as resolved, and any associated workout. For FeltIt Pro subscribers, a summary of each flare-up is also added. Notes are included only when Include Notes is enabled under Settings › Siri & Search in the application; this setting is disabled by default. Entries logged within the application may also be provided to iOS as suggestions for Siri shortcuts. The index and these suggestions are held by iOS on the device and are not transmitted to the developer.
  • Siri requests. When you ask Siri about your information or record an entry using Siri, the request is processed by Apple, and the response is composed on the device from your stored entries. Apple's processing of Siri requests is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. No request or response is transmitted to the developer.
  • In your iCloud account, where sync is enabled. When iCloud sync is enabled in the application's settings, entries and settings are synchronised through Apple's CloudKit service to the private database of your own Apple Account. This storage is under your control. The developer has no access to it. Apple's processing of that data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy. Where iCloud sync remains disabled, the information you enter is not transmitted from the device.

4. Anonymous usage statistics, and information not collected

FeltIt sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service, so that the developer can understand how the application is used: whether the introduction is completed, which features are used and how often, and where the FeltIt Pro screen is opened from. This is enabled by default and may be disabled at any time under Settings › About › Share Anonymous Usage. When it is disabled, the application records and sends nothing further to TelemetryDeck, not even a record that it was disabled, and discards any counts awaiting the next daily summary.

While it is enabled, the application sends:

  • Events describing use of the application: that the application was opened, and from which notification type where applicable; which introduction page was viewed; that Apple Health access was requested, and whether notifications were allowed; that the introduction was completed; that the first entry was saved; that the entry screen was opened, saved, or closed without saving, with where it was opened from, which step it was on, and whether a workout was attached; which tab was viewed; that History was searched or its filter changed, and to which filter; that a body region's detail, or the Capacity or Recovery explanation, was opened; that the FeltIt Pro screen was shown and from where; that plans could not be loaded; that a purchase was started, and how a purchase or restore ended (completed, pending, cancelled, failed, or whether a purchase was found); and that sharing was enabled again.
  • A daily summary, sent the next time the application is opened: how many times background refreshes ran, new workouts were synchronised from Apple Health, and entries, resolutions, or questions were made through Siri or Shortcuts; and current settings, namely whether FeltIt Pro is active and which plan, the theme, whether iCloud sync is enabled, which Apple Health data sources and notification types are enabled, and how many Insights sections are shown.
  • On a purchase of FeltIt Pro: the product, the type of purchase, whether it began a free trial, the price converted to US dollars, the currency, and the country of your App Store storefront; and, where a free trial later becomes a paid subscription, a signal noting that conversion.
  • Details the analytics service adds to every signal: the application version and build, whether the application was installed from the App Store or TestFlight, the device model, operating system version, screen size and orientation, language and region, time zone, the day and hour the signal was sent, whether the device is in light or dark mode, text size and other accessibility display settings (such as Bold Text, Reduce Motion, and Increase Contrast), how many days and sessions the application has been used and how long sessions last, and the date the application was first used.
  • The first time sharing begins: a signal from the analytics service noting a new installation.

These signals are associated only with a one-way hash of the identifier iOS assigns to applications from the same developer on your device. It is not linked to your name, Apple Account, or contact details, and TelemetryDeck states that it anonymises identifiers so that they cannot be traced back to a person. The signals never include the content of your entries: no body region, severity, sensation type, resolution, note, entry date, Capacity or Recovery figure, and no Apple Health data of any kind. TelemetryDeck states that it never stores IP addresses and that its servers are located in the European Union, in Germany and the Netherlands. The developer uses the statistics solely to understand and improve the application, and does not use them for advertising or tracking.

Apart from this, the application contains no advertising framework, crash-reporting software development kit, social login, or other third-party software library. Its own network activity consists of the anonymous usage statistics above, while they are enabled, and Apple's App Store verifying the price of FeltIt Pro and your purchase, described below. No server is operated by the developer in connection with the application. The developer does not receive your entries, notes, Apple Health data, or IP address, and cannot identify you from the usage statistics.

Health and fitness data is not used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is not sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

5. Purchases

FeltIt Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the transaction, and payment details are handled solely by Apple. They are not transmitted to, accessible by, or stored by the developer or the application. Purchase status is verified on your device through Apple's StoreKit framework.

Apple provides the developer with aggregated sales and financial reports covering units sold, revenue, and country. These reports do not identify individual customers.

While anonymous usage statistics are enabled, FeltIt also informs its analytics service when a purchase completes, with the details listed in section 4. It does not send your name, your Apple Account, or your payment details.

6. Crash reports and diagnostics

Where you have enabled the Share With App Developers setting under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements on your iPhone, Apple may provide the developer with crash logs and performance data relating to FeltIt. This information is collected and supplied by Apple rather than by the application, and Apple states that it is not associated with your identity. The setting may be disabled at any time in iOS.

The application maintains a diagnostic log on the device. It remains on the device and is not transmitted.

7. Notifications

All notifications issued by FeltIt — post-workout logging prompts, heads-up alerts, the morning briefing, and follow-up check-ins — are composed and scheduled on the device by iOS. Their content is not transmitted to any server. The application does not send marketing or promotional notifications.

8. Support correspondence

If you contact support by email, the developer receives your email address, the content of your message, and, where the Contact Support function within the application was used, the application version and build number, device model, and iOS version inserted automatically. This text is displayed for review and may be edited before sending.

Such correspondence is used solely to respond to your enquiry and is retained in the developer's email provider's mailbox. It is not used for any mailing list.

9. Managing and deleting your information

  • Individual entries may be edited or deleted within the application at any time.
  • Health access may be withdrawn in the Apple Health application under Sharing › Apps, or under Settings › Privacy & Security › Health. The application ceases reading new data immediately.
  • iCloud sync may be disabled in the application's settings, retaining information on the device only.
  • Anonymous usage statistics may be disabled under Settings › About › Share Anonymous Usage.
  • Notes may be excluded from the search index by disabling Include Notes under Settings › Siri & Search in the application. FeltIt may be removed from Siri and Spotlight entirely under iOS Settings › Apps › FeltIt › Siri.
  • Deleting the application removes its entire local store from the device.
  • The iCloud copy may be removed under iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

As no copy of this information is held by the developer, these steps constitute complete deletion. The anonymous usage statistics described in section 4 cannot be associated with you, and are addressed under Retention below.

10. Retention

Information is retained on your device, and in your iCloud account where sync is enabled, until you delete it. The application does not expire or automatically delete entries. No copy is retained by the developer.

Anonymous usage statistics are retained by TelemetryDeck. TelemetryDeck states that it has no fixed schedule for deleting stored signals, and that it expects to delete them after seven to ten years without guaranteeing it. As the statistics cannot be associated with an individual, the developer cannot locate and delete those relating to one person. Disabling Share Anonymous Usage stops new statistics from being sent.

11. Children

FeltIt is not directed at children and is not intended for use by persons under 13 years of age. The application sends only the anonymous usage statistics described in section 4, which do not identify anyone, and information from children is not knowingly collected.

12. Your rights

Canada. Under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to access personal information held about you by an organisation, to request correction of that information, and to make a complaint to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. The only personal information held by the developer is the content of support correspondence you elect to send; the anonymous usage statistics in section 4 cannot be associated with you.

United Kingdom and European Economic Area. Where you are located in the United Kingdom or the EEA, the UK GDPR and the GDPR provide rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and data portability, together with the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. As the application processes your entries and Apple Health data solely on your device and within your own iCloud account, these rights are exercised directly through the application and through iOS, including by disabling anonymous usage statistics (section 4).

California. Where you are a California resident, the CCPA and CPRA provide rights to know what personal information is collected, to request its deletion or correction, and not to be subject to discrimination for exercising those rights. The developer does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by that legislation. The anonymous usage statistics are described in section 4.

To exercise any of these rights in respect of support correspondence, contact developer.emadrahman@gmail.com.

13. Changes to this policy

Where FeltIt's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the date shown above revised. Material changes will additionally be noted in the application's release notes. The current version of this policy is accessible within the application under Settings › About › Privacy Policy.
